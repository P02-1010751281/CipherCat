# Supplementary Source Documents

[简体中文](./README.md)

This directory stores source PDFs cited by the research reports: the three NIST documents used by the randomness research, plus the ShannonProver paper used by the AI / formal-verification research. The index records official sources and SHA-256 values so local copies can be checked against the reviewed versions. Statistical-test results are diagnostic evidence only; they do not certify a random-number generator, and paper or repository information is research input rather than an endorsement.
