# Public Information on China’s Post-Quantum Cryptography

Checked: 2026-09-23. This page records located official public materials. It does not present research projects, draft standards, or research topics as published algorithm standards, nor infer that a type of standard does not exist merely because a search returned no result.

## Verified Official Materials

| Date | Document | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022-03-23 | [2022 Open-Topic Guide of the State Key Laboratory of Cryptology](https://oscca.gov.cn/sca/ztpd/2022-03/23/content_1060913.shtml) | The official research guide lists lattice-, code-, multivariate-, hash-, and isogeny-based post-quantum research topics. | It does not show that the Chinese Association for Cryptologic Research established a standardization working group in 2022, and it is not a list of standard candidates. |
| 2023-12-13 | [OSCCA Announcement No. 46](https://oscca.gov.cn/sca/xwdt/2023-12/13/content_1061150.shtml) | The announcement lists post-quantum cryptography and protocol research tasks; submitting protocol draft standards to standardization organizations is a project deliverable. | A research task or draft deliverable is not itself a formally published national or industry standard. |
| 2026-01-30 | [OSCCA Announcement No. 55](https://oscca.gov.cn/sca/xwdt/2026-01/30/content_1061322.shtml) | A new round of public research topics continues to include code-, lattice-, and other post-quantum cryptography research. | A research call alone does not establish whether an algorithm standard has been published. |

## Boundary of the Project’s Implementation

CipherCat’s implementation status for ML-KEM (FIPS 203) and ML-DSA (FIPS 204) is a project capability. Consult the corresponding standard pages, coverage matrix, and test results. This does not establish conformance to any Chinese standard or compatibility with a future or different algorithm standard.

This page tracks public Chinese post-quantum cryptography research and standardization materials. The U.S. National Security Agency’s Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite (CNSA) is outside its scope.
